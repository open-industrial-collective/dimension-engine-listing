A development preview for industrial visualization. Explore a synthetic process skid, a credited building capture, interactive facility and process diagrams, simulated plant scenarios, and Super Maintenance Bros., a playable WebGPU factory platformer with a guided demo, cinematic camera and FPS counter. Use Play in the showcase menu to run, jump and restart the factory. The hosted preview runs in a browser with synthetic or reference data; no plant or Ignition Gateway is connected. The development application can also run through Ignition Perspective and WebDev, but a native Dimension Engine module is not available yet.
